## How are Enemies defined?
- What all enemies have:
    - base stats from their race that scale with level
    - at least one skill
    - A base scoring formula that takes in all the deciding factors (Enemy HP, Player HP, etc), which can vary depending on which floor the enemy is on as well as what "rank" the enemy is, different races of enemy would value factors differently.
    - No enemy has the natural ability to see what action the player has chosen this round of combat

- Regular Enemies
    - The lowest ranking enemy in the dungeon which can be found on every floor in the dungeon, in there specified rooms
    - Regular Enemies have the lowest total base stats of the different "ranks" of enemies
    - Regular Enemies have only 1 skill
    - Regular Enemies have the simplest scoring system, only taking the base scoring system value and applying their race's valuing to the factors
    - Regular Enemies are given the knowledge of the Player's skills and loadout, factoring them into their decision from floor 70 onwards

- Mini Boss Enemies:
    - The middle ranking enemy in the dungeon which are found every 5th floor that isn't a multiple of 10, in a mini boss room which the player must clear before being avle to reach the next floor
    - Mini Bosses act like upgraded versions of regular enemies ("Chief Orc" and "Alpha Werewolf" as examples)
    - Mini Bosses have higher base stat totals than regular enemies but not as much as Zone Bosses
    - Mini Bosses have multiple skills, 2 skills < floor 25, 3 < floor 75, 4 skills onward
    - Mini Bosses take the scoring formula of their regular enemy counter part and is more refined, added checks, and overall an upgraded version
    - Mini Bosses are given the knowledge of the Player's skills and loadout, factoring them into their decision from floor 55 onwards
    - Mini Bosses from floor 35 onwards have a consumable item (simple items at first to more useful and stronger items later into the dungeon)

- Zone Boss Enemies:
    - The highest ranking enemy in the dungeon, which are found on the final floor of a "Zone" (every 10 floors) in a Zone Boss room which must be cleared before the player can go to the next floor
    - Zone Bosses aren't based on previous enemy "ranks" and are their own individual race
    - Zone Bosses have names as well
    - Zone Bosses have the highest base stat totals out of all the enemies in the dungeon
    - Zone Bosses have multiple skills (3-6)
    - Zone Bosses each have their own additions and how they value factors in the base scoring formula
    - Zone Bosses are given the knowledge of the Player's skills and loadout, factoring them into their decision from the start
    - Zone Bosses from floor 30 onwards have a consumable item (simple items at first to more useful and stronger items later into the dungeon)

- Secret Boss Enemies: (Secondary goal)
    - Late game bosses that are optional to the Player and can only be unlocked through pursuing side quests, they uncover more lore of the game that the player usually only gets scraps from through boss fights and rare rooms.
    - Secret Bosses are extremely unique in that they introduce brand-new concepts into the combat which the player wouldn't encounter otherwise
    - Secret Bosses have base stat totals which rival that of the Zone Bosses
    - Secret Bosses have multiple skills (4), yet these are more complex than the usual skills
    - Secret Bosses each have their own unqiue version of the base scoring formula that are refined to be the biggest challenge for the players alongside late Zone Bosses
    - Secret Bosses are given the knowledge of the Player's skills and loadout, factoring them into their decision
    - Secret Bosses have a high-level consumable item

- Zone refers to every 10 floors in the dungeon, each Zone has thematic motif and increase the amount of rooms per floor
